Greetings folks!

Thanks to our members' generous support, this is the 6th year we we will be awarding annual funding to 5 developers - paid in twelve $1,500 monthly stipends (for a total of $90,000 USD). In the past 5 years, we have seen that giving developers flexible, long-term funding gives them the space to do high-impact work. This might be continuing maintenance on existing projects, new feature development, or perhaps a brand-new project. 

On average, 93% of our members use work from our funded developers every day or on a regular basis. We’ve been excited with what they came up with in the last few years and are looking forward to seeing more great work in 2027! 

If you are not interested in applying, but know someone who should, please feel free to forward the application information to them. 

Thanks all and good luck! 

### PROCESS
**Apply:** Anyone interested in receiving annual funding submits the [**APPLICATION**](https://forms.gle/6kbiJTms2c2J97J3A) outlining what they intend to work on and how that work will benefit the Clojure community.  **The deadline for applications is Dec. 7, 2026 midnight Pacific Time. Please note that past grantees must re-apply each year.**  

**Board Review:** The Clojurists Together board will review the applications and select finalists to present to the members by **Dec. 9th**.  

**Members Vote:** The final ballot will go out to members using a [Ranked Vote](https://www.rankedvote.co/guides/understanding-ranked-choice-voting/how-does-ranked-choice-voting-work) election to determine the final recipients. As always, your input and participation is important and helps make Clojurists Together effective by ensuring members’ voices inform the work undertaken. **Deadline Dec. 18, 2026 midnight Pacific time**. 

**Awards will be announced no later than Dec. 28, 2026**.

**Project Updates:** Recipients are required to submit a report bi-monthly to the membership.
