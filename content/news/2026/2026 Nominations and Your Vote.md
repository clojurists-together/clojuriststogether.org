Clojurists Together Foundation is in the process of electing new board members as three of our existing board members are finishing their terms. One member, Lorelai Lyons, has submitted an application for another two year term.

## Departing members

Two others, Laurens Van Houtven (lvh) and Maria Geller have decided to step aside to open the door for other Clojurists to participate. Maria has been a board member since Clojurists Together was first formed in 2017, and lvh was elected in the first board election in 2018. lvh has been President of the Clojurists Together Foundation since we formed our own non-profit trade association in 2020 and put in a huge amount of time and energy to leave the Software Freedom Conservancy and form our own non-profit. I want to extend a very special thanks to them for their years of service and leadership in the community, and will be sad to see them leave. They were both instrumental in helping Clojurists Together get to where it is today.

## Election

After accepting applications for nominations, we received 5 applications from candidates - who will serve two-year terms. These candidates are all talented Clojurists that we would love to have on the board.  

**Our bylaws state:**

The Board shall nominate no more than 12 candidates seeking board membership in any given election. In nominating candidates for Director positions and in choosing the number of candidates to nominate overall, the Board shall use reasonable efforts to maintain a Board composition consisting of at least: (1) 25% female Directors, (2) 25% non-Caucasian Directors, and (3) 35% from any category(ies) of persons (e.g., race, gender, ability) commonly considered to have suffered from discrimination at some time and then-currently under-represented in the technology industry, in each case as determined by the Board in its reasonable discretion.  

The board has nominated the following 5 candidates for the current board election:  

Arthur Fücher   
Henning Jansen  
Lorelai Lyons  
Chris Oakman  
Mohit Verma  



###  Next Steps
All current Clojurists Together members will be receiving their ballot to vote shortly. If you haven’t received a ballot by Wednesday, 22nd October, please contact hi@clojuriststogether.org.

Voting will close on Monday, 9th November, 2026 at midnight Pacific Time. The top three candidates, as voted on by members will win. We will announce the winners shortly afterwards and you’ll be able to meet them at the Annual members meeting at 10 am Pacific time, Nov. 17th, 2026. The new board will meet shortly after the annual meeting to select officers.
