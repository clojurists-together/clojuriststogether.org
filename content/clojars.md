[Clojars](https://clojars.org/) is the community repository for open source
Clojure libraries. It gives maintainers a free place to publish their work and
gives Clojure developers and build tools a dependable place to retrieve it.

Clojars is a member project of the Clojurists Together Foundation. Keeping this
shared infrastructure reliable, secure, and available is part of our mission to
support the open source software and infrastructure that the Clojure community
depends on.

## Supporting Clojars maintenance

Clojurists Together funds [Toby Crawley](https://github.com/tobias) (lead maintainer) and
[Daniel Compton](https://github.com/danielcompton) (maintainer) to maintain Clojars. In
2026, this is a combined long-term grant of $1,500 USD per month.

Their work includes responding to operational issues and community support
requests, maintaining the service and its infrastructure, applying security
updates, improving publishing and administration tools, and keeping hosting
reliable and cost-effective. You can follow this work in the
[Clojars changelog](https://github.com/clojars/clojars-web/blob/main/CHANGELOG.org)
and in our [project updates](/news/).

## Critical infrastructure for the Clojure community

Clojars has provided free JAR hosting to the Clojure community since November
2009. As of October 2026:

* Clojars [hosts](https://clojars.org/projects) more than 33,000 projects with 230,000 versions released.
* Those versions had been downloaded more than **3.3 billion times**.

## Get help or contribute

For help with Clojars, visit the [`#clojars` channel on Clojurians
Slack](https://clojurians.slack.com/archives/C0H28NMAS) or
[open an issue](https://github.com/clojars/clojars-web/issues/new/choose).
Clojars itself is open source, and contributions are welcome in the
[Clojars GitHub organization](https://github.com/clojars).
